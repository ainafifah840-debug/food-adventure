🍎 For Teachers: Curriculum Alignment & Design Notes

Instructional objectives

By the end of the lesson, pupils will be able to:

  1. Listening / vocabulary strategy: guess the meaning of at least three unfamiliar food-related words from clues provided by other known words (LS 1.3.1).
  2. Speaking – describing: describe a food item using at least three suitable statements about its colour, shape, taste or features (LS 2.1.5).
  3. Speaking – narrating: narrate a short basic story or event about a meal or food-related experience using simple, sequenced sentences (LS 2.3.1).
  4. Listening extension: identify TRUE or FALSE information after listening to Sarah's healthy-eating interview. This is additional listening practice and is not mapped to one of the three LS codes supplied.

Content Standards

  • 1.3 Use appropriate listening strategies in a variety of contexts.
  • 2.1 Communicate simple information intelligibly.
  • 2.3 Communicate appropriately to a small or large group.

Learning Standards

  • 1.3.1 Guess the meaning of unfamiliar words from clues provided by other known words.
  • 2.1.5 Describe people, places, and objects using suitable statements.
  • 2.3.1 Narrate short basic stories and events.

Multimedia modes

  • Visual food illustrations, flashcards, icons, colour-coded sections.
  • Linguistic vocabulary, contextual clues, Malay translations, example sentences.
  • Audio supports pre-recorded narration when audio files are supplied, with browser text-to-speech as a fallback.
  • Spatial consistent card layout, grouped content, clear navigation.
  • Gestural clicking, flipping, selecting, matching and navigating.
  • Digital animated flashcards, instant feedback, scoring and badge progress.

Design principles

Multimedia learning: words and pictures sit together, content is split into small steps, and pupils control the pace. CASPER: strong colour contrast, aligned cards, simple screens, related items grouped close together, key words emphasised and the same layout repeated in every section. Accessibility: large fonts, keyboard navigation, visible focus outlines, text alternatives and short Bahasa Melayu support lines.

Privacy: no accounts, no log-in and no personal information. Progress badges are saved only in this browser and can be cleared on the completion page.

📝 Describe and Tell a Short Story

Describe (LS 2.1.5): Choose a food and describe it using at least three suitable statements. You can talk about its colour, shape, taste and texture.

Example: “A mango is a yellow or orange fruit. It is oval-shaped. It tastes sweet and juicy.”

Narrate (LS 2.3.1): Tell your partner about a meal or food-related event. Say what happened first, next and last.

Example: “Yesterday, I had dinner with my family. First, we prepared the food. Then, we ate rice and chicken together. After dinner, we had watermelon.”
